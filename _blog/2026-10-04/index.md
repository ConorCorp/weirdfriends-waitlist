Have a new update out for yas containing 2 big changes.

## dark mode 🌙
My wife, Laura <3, added dark mode to the app with a theme that is perfect for Spooky Season 🎃 It took a little bit more work than I'd expected but am very happy with the result. Thank you to Michelle for sending us an email asking for it!

## chats is now the home page 💬
I wanted to reduce friction out so that you guys can just get chatting as quickly as possible, so now the old homepage was removed, and your dropped right into the chats screen.

## what's next 🤔
I'm not sure yet, but I want to make the app more fun, not require typing always, and especially more generative without having to prompt the bots to do things always.

I've been toying around with a few options, and I built a demo of a very interesting idea I can't share yet, but keep an eye out for it!

I'd ideally like to add message streaming with a web socket/sse connection to the backend as well so messages stream quicker.

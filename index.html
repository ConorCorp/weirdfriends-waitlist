faq

questions people text us

What is weirdfriends?
Think character.ai for language learners. weirdfriends is a free language learning app for iOS and Android where you learn by texting AI characters. Each friend has a personality (a ghost pirate, an alien anthropologist, a microwave in a Wichita break room) and texts you in the language you're learning. Tap any message for a translation, tap any mistake for a correction, and optionally let your friends message you first throughout the day. You can also create your own characters.
Is weirdfriends free?
Yes, completely, and right now usage is unlimited. Every language, every character, corrections, translations, scenes and notifications are free. No trial, no credit card. Paid plans are coming soon, so this is the moment to get in and use it as much as you like.
Which languages can I learn?
Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Romanian, Polish, Greek, Ukrainian, Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay and English. Each has its own page under languages.
How is weirdfriends different from Duolingo?
Duolingo teaches you a language through short structured lessons and exercises. weirdfriends skips the lessons and drops you straight into conversation with characters who have personalities, correcting you as you go. There's no curriculum, you can talk about anything, you can invent your own characters, and your friends text you first. Lots of people use both: a course for the rules, weirdfriends for actually using them. Full comparison here.
Can I create my own AI character?
Yes. Give your friend a name, an emoji and a short personality description (at least 20 characters, the more detail the better) and they'll text you in any of the supported languages, in character. You can edit them later. More on creating friends.
Do I have to turn on notifications?
No. Notifications are optional. If you turn them on, your friends will message you throughout the day in the language you're learning, in character, so practice feels like texting a mate. If you turn them off, nothing changes except the nudges.
What level do I need to be?
Any level from complete beginner (A1) to fluent (C2). You set your level and your friends adjust how they text. It shines from A2 or B1 upwards, when you can form basic sentences and need somewhere to actually use them.
Are my conversations private?
Yes. Every conversation is private, just you and your friends. See the privacy policy for details.
Does it do speaking or voice?
Not yet. weirdfriends is text chat right now, on purpose: texting is how most people use a second language day to day, and it lets you take your time. Voice is on the roadmap.
Where can I get it?
On the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

faq

creating friends

Is creating a character free?
Yes. Make as many as you like. Creating, editing and chatting with your own friends is all part of the free app.
What should I write in the personality?
Who they are, what they want, how they talk, and anything they're weird about. At least 20 characters; a few sentences is ideal. "You're a witty space explorer who speaks in riddles and loves obscure trivia" is the in-app example. You can edit it any time.
Which languages can my character speak?
All of them. Your friend isn't tied to one language; you pick the language per chat. The same tortoise can text you in Danish on Monday and Chinese on Tuesday. See all 24 languages.
Can my character be flirty, raunchy or sarcastic?
They'll match the personality you give them: flirty, raunchy, dramatic, sarcastic, grumpy, absurd, wholesome. Conversations are private, it's just you and your friend. Keep it kind, and have fun.
Will my character still correct my mistakes?
Yes. Corrections and translations work the same with your own friends as with the hand-picked ones: mistakes underlined, tap for the fix and the explanation, +10 for perfect messages.
Can my created friend message me first?
Yes, if you have notifications on. They'll text you through the day, in character, in whichever language you've been chatting in.

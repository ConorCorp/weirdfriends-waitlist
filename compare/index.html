weirdfriends vs Duolingo most asked

Duolingo is a gamified course: short lessons, streaks, leagues, a huge language list. It's very good at getting you to show up and at teaching you how the language is put together.

weirdfriends has no lessons. You text characters with personalities, about anything, and get corrected as you go. It's for the moment after the lessons, when you can conjugate but can't hold a conversation. It also does two things Duolingo doesn't: lets you invent your own characters, and has those characters message you first, in the language, instead of a streak reminder.

use Duolingo to learn the rules and build a habit. use weirdfriends to actually talk. Plenty of people run both; they don't overlap much.

weirdfriends vs Babbel

Babbel is a structured, subscription-based course built around practical dialogues and grammar explanations. It's more "classroom" than Duolingo and good for learners who want a syllabus.

weirdfriends is the opposite shape: no syllabus, free, and all conversation. Babbel's dialogues are scripted; weirdfriends' aren't, so you have to actually produce the language instead of recognising it. Corrections come with explanations, and you decide what to talk about.

use Babbel if you want a course with a teacher's voice. use weirdfriends for the unscripted practice a course can't give you.

weirdfriends vs ChatGPT

ChatGPT can absolutely chat with you in Spanish, and lots of people try. The catch: it isn't built for learning. You have to prompt it to stay in character, prompt it to correct you, prompt it to translate, and it never texts you first. A persona you set up lives in one conversation and is gone from the next.

weirdfriends bakes all of that in. Characters persist and remember the bit. Every message is checked by a dedicated correction model; mistakes are underlined and tap-to-explain. Any message translates with a tap. Your level (A1–C2) shapes how they write. And your friends slide into your DMs on their own.

use ChatGPT as a grammar reference. use weirdfriends for daily practice that doesn't need prompting.

weirdfriends vs a tutor (italki, Preply)

A human tutor is the best language practice money can buy: real speaking, real listening, real feedback, a real relationship. It also costs money per hour, needs scheduling, and can be intimidating when you're at the "I know I'm making mistakes" stage.

weirdfriends is the thing between sessions. Free, text-based, available at 2am, zero judgment. Say something ridiculous to a ghost pirate, get corrected, try again. Then bring the confidence to your next lesson.

get a tutor if you can; nothing beats it for speaking. use weirdfriends for the daily reps in between, or if lessons aren't in the budget right now.

comparison questions

Is weirdfriends a Duolingo alternative?
For conversation practice, yes. If you want a structured course with lessons, Duolingo is still that, and weirdfriends isn't. Most people who switch were already past the lessons and stuck at "I can't actually talk". Many keep both.
Can weirdfriends replace a tutor?
For speaking, no: it's text-based. For daily written practice, feedback on your mistakes and the confidence to say things out loud later, it covers a lot of what people hire tutors for, at no cost.
Why not just use ChatGPT to practise a language?
You can. weirdfriends is what you'd build if you did that every day for a year: characters that persist, automatic corrections with explanations, tap-to-translate, level-aware replies, and friends who message you first. No prompting required.
Is weirdfriends really free?
Yes, and right now usage is unlimited. No trial, no credit card. All languages, characters, corrections, translations and notifications are free. Paid plans are coming soon, so get in while it's free.
What makes weirdfriends different from other AI language apps?
Three things: you can create your own characters and they stay in character; the characters text you first so practice happens without scheduling; and the whole thing is built to be fun rather than school-shaped. Flirty, absurd, dramatic, mundane: you choose the vibe.
